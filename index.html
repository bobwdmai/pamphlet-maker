4 How to print & fold it

    How Book Maker works

    Why pages get reordered

    Real books are printed as folded, stapled sheets ("saddle-stitch"). Two pages share one side of a sheet, but not in reading order: the first sheet carries the first and last pages, the next sheet the second and second-to-last, and so on inward. Folding and stapling the stack is what puts everything back into 1, 2, 3… order — this tool works out that order for you.

    Getting your file from Google Docs

    File → Download → PDF Document (.pdf). Each Doc page becomes one PDF page, which is what this tool expects.

    Paper & layout

    Pick the paper size actually loaded in your printer. Each sheet prints in landscape with two book pages side by side, then folds down the middle.

    Long documents don't fold well as one giant booklet. This tool automatically splits them into signatures — smaller, evenly-sized booklets — that you fold, staple, and gather together, the same way real bound books are made.

    Each page is scaled down uniformly to fit its half of the sheet — text and images shrink together, since there's no way to keep text full-size while only shrinking images (that would mean re-typesetting the document, not imposing it). If your source document is a full Letter/A4 page, expect a noticeable shrink even on larger paper. For full-size text, set your source document's page size to roughly half the sheet size (e.g. 5.5 × 8.5 in for a Letter sheet) before exporting — the standard approach for self-published books.

    No auto-duplex printer?

    Check "My printer can't auto-duplex" to also get separate front/back files for manual double-sided printing, plus a one-sheet calibration tool to work out the right rotation/order combination for your printer before committing the whole job to paper.

    Creep compensation

    In a thick booklet, nested sheets push inner pages' content slightly toward the outer edge as they fold. Turning on creep compensation nudges each sheet's content a little further from the spine the deeper it sits in the stack, to counteract that.

    Privacy

    Everything runs locally in your browser using pdf-lib. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
